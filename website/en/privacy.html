Privacy policy

Updated: November 16, 2026

This policy covers the ClipDock Windows and Mac apps and its official website. ClipDock saves video and audio to your PC from URLs you provide. ClipDock has no proprietary usage reporting, advertising, analytics or telemetry.

The app does not read existing Chrome or Edge cookies, saved login information or past browsing history.

The login window, browser tab and storage details below describe Windows. See Differences on Mac for the Mac version.

Login information within the app

“Use login information for this download” is on by default and retained locally. Turning it off prevents login cookies being passed to the engine for the next added or retried job. The dedicated window remains signed in.

Use Login to sign in to the site you want to save from in a dedicated WebView2 window. Its profile is separate from Chrome and Edge. Information entered on login pages is sent to the relevant site. Password autosave and general autofill are disabled.

The dedicated profile's cookies are also checked to display login status. When the option is on, a download or retry exports only cookies for its target site to a temporary engine file. Cookie values are excluded from download history and removed from engine error displays.

“Delete login information” clears all sites’ login information and browsing data in ClipDock. It cannot be used while jobs are active; finish or cancel them first. Pages opened inside the app are also subject to their sites' terms and privacy policies.

Optional browser tab

The browser tab is off by default. When enabled, the app checks the type and position of the foreground Chrome or Edge window and the pointer position. Pointer movement history is not stored. Turning it off stops these checks.

The address bar is read only when you click the tab. Windows UI Automation reads a URL candidate and places it in the app. No download starts until you check the URL and click Save.

This feature does not read page content, cookies, browsing history or the clipboard. It does not continuously collect URLs. You can paste a URL into the app instead.

Your display language is also stored in the settings file.

Temporary cookie files are deleted after completion, failure or cancellation. If deletion fails or the app exits unexpectedly, deletion is attempted again at the next startup.

Clear history removes completed, failed and cancelled records and their unfinished work files. It does not delete completed media or login information. Uninstalling leaves settings, history, the login profile and saved files. To remove all app data, close the app and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipDock, unwanted media and any remaining .clipdock-work folders in your destinations.

Differences on Mac

The Mac version handles the same information for the same purposes and connects to the same services as the Windows version. Storage locations and protection differ as follows.

  • Settings, history and the download engine are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/ClipDock. History is not encrypted; access to the folder and files is limited to the current user.
  • The Login window uses the WebKit engine built into macOS. Cookies and browsing data are kept in WebKit data that belongs to the app (such as ~/Library/WebKit/app.clipdock.ClipDock) and are not shared with Safari or other browsers.
  • “Delete login information” removes the cookies of every site in ClipDock.
  • There is no optional browser tab. The Mac version does not read other apps' windows or address bars.
  • Moving the app to the Trash leaves settings, history, login data and saved files in place.

Network connections and use

Downloads send the URL, referrer, engine User-Agent and other retrieval information to media providers and sites needed for page analysis. When login use is enabled, cookies also authenticate requests to the corresponding site. Those sites receive your IP address.

The app prepares and updates its engine automatically at launch, retrieving distribution files and license information from GitHub. The dedicated login window connects to HTTPS sites you open and the pages needed for authentication. It uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime on Windows and WebKit on Mac.

User data is not sold or used for advertising, credit assessment or analysis unrelated to saving media. The developer has no mechanism to retrieve your download history or cookies.

Website and downloads

The official website, guides, help pages and installer are delivered through Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP address, requested URL and browser information for delivery and security. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

The website contains no custom analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The app does not send download history or login information to the website.
